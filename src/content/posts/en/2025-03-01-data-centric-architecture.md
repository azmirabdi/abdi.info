IT has advanced far enough that even traditional organisations can align their business models consistently around data. But this is about more than digitalisation. It is about simplifying the digital flow of data, and orchestrating it centrally, so that it produces as much business value as it can:

- **Less complexity and lower cost**
- **Faster time to market**
- **Knowledge management that is native to AI**
- **New data-driven uses**
- **Regulatory requirements that are easier to meet**
- **Transparency through digital twins**

**Utopia or reality?**

One thing is clear: the path so far no longer holds. After SOA, microservices, and rising digitalisation, we have reached a complexity that can be controlled only with immense effort.

New solutions are needed. Low-code and no-code platforms offer approaches. How do they fit with data sovereignty, GDPR compliance, independence from providers, open source, knowledge graphs, AI integration, and digital twins?

**The answer: data-driven digitalisation with full sovereignty**

Organisations need an architecture that makes digital transformation possible without creating dependencies. A solution that keeps data safely in their own hands, gives employees simple access to information that already exists, and integrates AI natively, while keeping it strictly separate from sensitive company data.

**The data-centric approach** is that solution. Implemented as an open, scalable architecture, it lets organisations stand independent of vendors, keep the focus on their own core business, and secure an advantage through data-driven uses.

**The principles of data-centric architecture:**

- **DATA AVAILABILITY** → data in real time, independent of applications
- **DATA OWNERSHIP** → clear responsibility for domain components
- **DATA SEMANTICS** → context through metadata and relationships
- **DATA SOVEREIGNTY** → full control, a strict separation from AI models
- **DATA CATALOGUE** → findability and self-service use
- **DATA SECURITY** → protection classes and deliberate access control
- **DATA SCALABILITY** → real-time access independent of peak load
- **DATA QUALITY** → validation and governance through data contracts
- **DATA MONETIZATION** → measurable value created from data

**Make or buy? The strategic decision**

While core IT processes have so far been built individually, a data-centric architecture changes that dynamic. The key is not proprietary applications. It is a flexible data platform that enables an organisation to act independently, to stand apart from competitors, and to react with agility to changes in the market through AI and data-driven business models.

Standardising data creates a new category of buy-products that can be integrated directly into the organisation’s own platform, without giving away control of valuable company data. Knowledge and data stay inside. AI becomes usable natively. The data-driven shift becomes real.
