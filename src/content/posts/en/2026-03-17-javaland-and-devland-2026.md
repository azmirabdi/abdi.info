"Sexism is something I experience every week."

That sentence is rendered from the German spoken on the panel. It is not a transcript. It came from the Women in Tech discussion, and it stayed with me for the rest of the conference.

JavaLand and DevLand ran back to back in mid-March at Europa-Park in Rust. JavaLand from 10 to 12 March 2026, DevLand on 12 and 13 March. My talk was at DevLand: "The Birth of the Data-Centric App".

As a father, the sentence gave me particular pause. In 2026, women in Germany are still judged too often by appearance or gender, rather than by what they can do.

I immigrated to Germany about 25 years ago, from a country strongly shaped by communist ideas. Equality and brotherhood were, at least, an important social ideal there. That is why it still surprises me that this subject remains so present in a country that is, in principle, very free.

Diversity is not a box I tick because I am supposed to. It means more perspectives, and so better decisions. Different experiences and ways of looking help us build solutions that are more robust, more creative, and of higher quality, especially in technology.

Alongside that, there was a great deal of tech.

The talk was there to show that it does not have to be that hard to introduce AI, to build a clear data strategy, and to reduce the complexity of IT. With small language models, semantic data products, and data-centric principles, business domains can provide their own data, more efficiently and more sustainably.

The question on the slide was plain. Can I, in good conscience, sell this product today? Demand, the supply chain, price, compliance, the signals from support. In an application-centric landscape the app collects all of that itself, harmonises it, and builds the next silo along the way. A data-centric app takes the insights that domains have already published as data products. I had filled in the canvas for that live for the first time in September, at the [Big Bang AI Festival](/en/blog/2025/semantic-data-products-at-the-big-bang-ai-festival/). The architecture around it is in [Data-Centric Architecture](/en/blog/2025/data-centric-architecture/).

AI is energy-intensive. That is true. Used properly, it can also be part of the solution.

DevLand came right after JavaLand, in the same park. Very visionary. Very inspiring. If we put only 10 percent of the ideas presented there into practice, I am not worried about Germany as a place for technology.

Thank you for the conversations, the talks, and the encounters with so many fascinating people. I will be back next year.
