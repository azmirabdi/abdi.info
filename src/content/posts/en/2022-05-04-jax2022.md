[JAX 2022](https://jax.de/mainz/programm-mainz/) back in Mainz, in person: a small piece of the old normality inside the new reality. It does you good to talk again, directly, with enthusiastic IT people about the topics that are moving. Topics such as #Kubernetes, #Serverless, #DDD, #jMolecules, #Quarkus, #Knative, #WebAssembly, #MutationTesting, and so on. From the big-picture flyover to the deep-dive live coding, there is a lot in it for an IT soul.

I was also able to give a talk with my colleague @Winfried Bittner, on **“Event-Driven Microservices-Tests”**: [https://jax.de/devops-continuous-delivery/event-driven-microservices-tests-wie-die-cd-pipeline-mit-cdc-und-bdd-die-e2e-tests-abloest/](https://jax.de/devops-continuous-delivery/event-driven-microservices-tests-wie-die-cd-pipeline-mit-cdc-und-bdd-die-e2e-tests-abloest/)

Everything around #ContinuousDelivery meets more and more interest. Our everyday IT work keeps getting more volatile. The talk offers a few aids for placing testing strategies in an agile setting.

Thanks to the speakers, to the organiser Sandsmedia GmbH, and to colleagues at sidion GmbH for the organisation.

Thanks for your time. Warm regards,
