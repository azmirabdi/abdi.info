When is my company actually data-driven? And do I have to buy an AI platform for that?

My answer is no.

David Koenig invited me onto DOAG Voices to talk about it, episode 42, CloudTalk. Recorded at CloudLand, out at the beginning of September. The episode is called "Daten nutzbar machen, KI-Agenten befreien". Episode 42. The answer to every question.

What I said there is the same as in the talk. Data belongs where it comes into being, as a product of the domain, with a meaning an agent can read. And the agent itself runs on the platform that is already there. Kubernetes, GitOps, a container. The second AI platform can wait. I wrote the talk up in [Freeing the AI Agents](/en/blog/2026/freeing-the-ai-agents/).

Thanks to David. If these questions are on your mind as well, have a listen.
