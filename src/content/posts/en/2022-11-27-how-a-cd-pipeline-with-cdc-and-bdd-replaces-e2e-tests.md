A careful combination of behaviour-driven development (BDD) and consumer-driven contracts (CDC) can carry later end-to-end tests, following the shift-left idea, inside one’s own CD pipelines. We present one approach to that.

This is an article I was able to publish with my colleague Winfried Bittner in Java Magazin.

The full article, later published on the [entwickler.de](https://entwickler.de/testing/testingpipeline-cdc-bdd) platform, is here: [https://entwickler.de/testing/testingpipeline-cdc-bdd](https://entwickler.de/testing/testingpipeline-cdc-bdd)
