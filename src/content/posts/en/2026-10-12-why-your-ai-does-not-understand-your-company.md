What is a RAG silo? And what is better kept out of a system prompt?

That was the subject on 8 October 2026 at data2day, at the KOMED in Cologne. The talk was called "Warum eure KI euer Unternehmen nicht versteht". With knowledge graphs, from the RAG silo to the semantic mesh. My first data2day.

A knowledge graph makes domain knowledge structured and readable by machines. Onboarding gets easier, so does covering for someone, and the knowledge does not hang on one person. Along the way a domain ontology comes into being. It can be reused for semantic data products and for AI agents.

Done the way the semantic mesh intends, that becomes a federated semantic layer for the whole company. Without a central monolith.

The beginning is plain. Domain knowledge does not belong in ever longer system prompts. The prompt steers how the agent behaves. The graph supplies the knowledge and the context. A fatter prompt, a skill, another document in a private RAG pipeline: that works. Afterwards the knowledge sits in exactly one use case. The next one starts again.

I had set out the idea of running agents on the platform that is already there in May, at [CloudLand](/en/blog/2026/freeing-the-ai-agents/).
