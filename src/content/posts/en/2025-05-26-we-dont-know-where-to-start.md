In conversations with clients about IT architectures for a data-driven organisation, I often hear sentences like these:

1. “The topic is far too big.”
2. “We don’t know where or how to start.”
3. “That isn’t our responsibility at all.”
4. “The C-level has to decide that first.”
5. “We don’t have time for a fundamental rebuild.”
6. “More important topics are waiting. The quarter is closing.”
7. … pick one.

And yes: a transformation toward a data-driven organisation that is driven purely **bottom-up** rarely reaches its aim.
**But** a pure **top-down** strategy is not enough either.

**Often the C-level first has to see that there is willingness in the workforce, and that it can also be simple and pragmatic.** Then something starts to move.

---

### My appeal to data platform owners, data architects, enterprise architects, and the people responsible for IT:

Show your C-level **that you can do it.**
That it does not have to be a mammoth project.
Build a small prototype. Show that it works.

---

### How do I get to a data-centric architecture?

**1. Formulate a vision.**
What do you hope for from a data-driven organisation? How does that fit the company strategy?
The idea: data is captured, processed, analysed, and made available as **data products**.

See also: [We have DevBizOps and DevSecOps. It is time for DevDataOps](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/wir-haben-devbizops-und-devsecops-jetzt-ist-es-zeit-f%C3%BCr-azmir-abdi-ojodf/?trackingId=w75nkIJJQG2vXy0AUJqMFA%3D%3D)

**2. Define clear principles.**
Examples:

- Prefer open source
- Our own data means no vendor lock-in
- Tools that deliver value quickly
- Security and privacy by design

See also: [Data-centric architecture: the key to digital transformation](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/datenzentrierte-architektur-der-schl%C3%BCssel-zur-digitalen-azmir-abdi-td4ae/?trackingId=XffTnOQ4QOa1cWwn7R1r%2BA%3D%3D)

**3. Start with a small use that still has an effect.**
My recommendation: **“Talk to Your Data”**
Why?

- You already need parts of a data-centric architecture
- The use can be built quickly
- The value is visible immediately

→ Connect a few source systems through a semantic knowledge graph.
→ Put a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) system on top.
→ Done. A result you can point at.

**4. Show the vision inside the organisation.**
Present your results. Show **that it works.**
You have proved that it can be done. Now it is the C-level’s turn.

Because later, nobody should be able to claim **that it was IT’s fault** if we in Europe, in Germany, do not move forward in the use of AI.

---

**In short:**
Stay with it. We have a chance to **set up an outdated IT location anew.**
And that can be done **step by step, with an eye on cost, and with a long view.**
